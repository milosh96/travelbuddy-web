# Travel Planner: Owlio

> An iOS app, free to try, that builds a day-by-day trip itinerary around your real
> arrival and departure times, so the first and last day get the hours they actually
> have instead of being counted as full days.

Owlio is made by Milos Cubrilo. It runs on iPhone and iPad, iOS 15.6 or later. There is
no Android version. No account is required to generate a plan.

## How it works

You enter one city, your arrival and departure times, and a pace (relaxed, balanced or
packed). Owlio returns a plan for each day: what to see, when, and in what order, with
every stop grouped and numbered on a map. Stops can be reordered, removed or added to.

Owlio plans one city per trip by design, which keeps each day walkable rather than
spread across a country.

## What the app includes

- Time-aware itineraries built around real arrival and departure times
- Every stop for a day grouped and numbered on an interactive map
- Boarding passes, hotel confirmations and tickets stored alongside the plan
- Landmark and painting identification through the camera
- Nearby restaurants, cafes and nightlife, ranked
- Daily weather forecast next to each day
- A record of the countries you have visited
