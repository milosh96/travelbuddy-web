Cookies and Analytics on the Website

The owlio.online website uses Google Analytics 4, provided through Firebase Analytics, to count visits and understand which pages are useful. This is the only analytics tool on the website and it is the only reason cookies are set. No advertising, profiling or cross-site tracking cookies are used, and the Service Provider does not sell or share your data with anyone for advertising.

Analytics does not run until you accept it. On your first visit a banner asks for your choice, and Google Analytics is not loaded and no cookie is written unless you select “Accept”. If you select “Decline”, or simply close the banner, no connection to Google is made at all — closing the banner is never treated as consent. Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage under owlio_consent_v1 and is remembered for 12 months, after which you will be asked again. You can change your mind at any time using the “Cookie settings” link in the website footer.

If you accept, the following cookies are set on the owlio.online domain:

  • _ga — assigns a pseudonymous identifier so returning visits are not double-counted. Expires 2 years after it is set or last updated.
  • _ga_TXN8X8PLDX — maintains the analytics session state for this property. Expires 2 years after it is set or last updated.

Google processes this data as a processor on the Service Provider's behalf, under Google's privacy policy. Where analytics is used, the legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you may withdraw at any time without affecting processing carried out beforehand. You can also block these cookies through your browser settings or by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Website analytics data is retained for 14 months.
